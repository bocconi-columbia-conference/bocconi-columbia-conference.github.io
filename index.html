About

Bocconi University and Columbia University host an annual conference in political science and political economy. The conference began in 2025 and alternates between the two institutions, meeting in Milan one year and in New York the next. The third conference will be held at Bocconi University on 8–9 June 2027.

We welcome submissions from scholars working on a wide range of questions in political science, economics, and related disciplines, including theoretical and empirical work. We also welcome papers that use quantitative, experimental, computational, or other data-driven methods to make substantive contributions.

The conference is aimed at academic researchers including faculty and postdoctoral researchers. PhD students can also apply to present in a short snippet session.

Submissions

Proposals can be an extended abstract of at least 500 words or a full paper, submitted through the submission form by 15 December 2026. There is no participation fee. We arrange accommodation and cover travel costs for presenters and discussants, in line with the CEPR Travel Guidelines.
